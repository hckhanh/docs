| `blockedWindows` | `AlertScheduleRestrictionBlockedWindow (input)[]` | yes      | Blocked time windows, half-open `[start, end)`. Windows must not overlap or touch, except that one may end at `00:00` where another starts. A window may wrap midnight (`end` before `start`), but only as the sole window, and two windows meeting at midnight are only allowed alongside a third.
